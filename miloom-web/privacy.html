Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

At Miloom, accessible from miloom.co and our iOS mobile application, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Miloom and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you:

  • Personal Data: While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including your email address, full name, and business entity details.
  • Financial Data: Through integrations like Plaid, we retrieve account balances, transaction history, and financial institution information that you explicitly choose to connect.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information on how the Service is accessed and used. This may include your device type, IP address, operating system, and system performance metrics.

2. How We Use Your Information

Miloom uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the Command Center platform.
  • To allow your secure business data to sync across your authenticated devices.
  • To provide customer support and process feedback.
  • To monitor the performance and stability of the application.
  • To notify you about updates, features, or administrative alerts.

3. Security of Data

The security of your data is extremely important to us. We use industry-standard encryption practices, secure cloud storage providers (such as Supabase), and secure authentication flows (such as Apple/Google Sign In and cryptographically signed biometric tokens) to protect your company data.

Remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service, to provide the Service on our behalf, or to assist us in analyzing how our Service is used. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@miloom.co.
